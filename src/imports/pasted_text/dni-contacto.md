Continue the existing redesign of the official website of the Dirección Nacional de Inteligencia (DNI) of the Dominican Republic.

This is NOT a new website concept.

Continue the existing DNI website that has already been designed and preserve its established design system, visual identity, navigation, header, footer, typography, spacing, components, colors and responsive behavior.

The two pages to create in this task are:

1. CONTACTO
2. SISTEMA NACIONAL DE INTELIGENCIA

Both pages must look like natural extensions of the existing DNI website.

========================================================
GENERAL DESIGN REQUIREMENTS
========================================================

Maintain the visual direction established in the DNI project brief:

- Minimalist
- Sober
- Institutional
- Modern
- Accessible
- Professional
- Trustworthy
- Technological without looking futuristic or excessive

Use the established DNI visual language:

- Dark navy blue
- Steel gray
- White/light backgrounds
- Gold accents
- Dominican national identity
- Existing DNI logo
- Official Dominican Republic identity elements
- Existing typography system

The design must communicate:

Authority + transparency + proximity.

The central institutional communication concept from the brief is:

"Protegemos lo que más te importa"

The pages should reinforce this concept without repeating it unnecessarily.

Use formal but accessible language.

Avoid unnecessary technical jargon.

Use active and citizen-oriented language.

Do not make the website feel like a generic technology company.

It must clearly feel like an official Dominican government intelligence institution.

========================================================

Use the DNI project brief as the primary source.

Do not invent:

- Addresses
- Telephone numbers
- Email addresses

- Government institutions
- Legal provisions
- Statistics
- Operational intelligence information
- Sensitive intelligence procedures
